For this assignment, I wanted to re-create audio visualization graphics. I had planned for the animation would be time-based and for it to not be based on any actual audio, which is how it is executed in the final product. Each column of animated rectangles are made up of no more than 6 rectangles, but no less than 1, which is coded via a for loop. The color palette was to remain within a specified pink/purple/blue palette, which was executed by laying transparent RISO colors on top of each other, effectively mixing up each respective color. Overall, the piece came out fairly close to how it was originally intended. The fast rhythm of the animated rectangles are meant to reflect loud bass, which would commonly be found in EDM/hyperpop music.
